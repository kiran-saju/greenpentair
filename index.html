About Green Pentair

Manufacturing experience.
Personal attention.

Established in 2016, Green Pentair is a manufacturer based in Kollam, Kerala. Our range includes graphic printed non-woven bags, D-cut bags, shopping bags, BOPP laminated bags, loop handle bags and printed carry bags.

We serve wholesale and retail customers with packaging options for different business requirements. Our manufacturing range also includes paper straw making machines.

Under the leadership of Mr. Vishnu, our experienced team focuses on understanding customer requirements and supplying products at competitive prices.
